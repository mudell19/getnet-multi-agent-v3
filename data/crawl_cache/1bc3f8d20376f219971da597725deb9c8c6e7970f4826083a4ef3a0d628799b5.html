Getnet expands its payments platform across Europe

  • Europe
  • Digital Payments

Getnet will offer payment solutions across over 30 countries in Europe, combining a single integration, a comprehensive suite of functionalities and dedicated  support.

Getnet, Santander’s global merchant payments platform, today announces the expansion of its payment business across Europe, enabling large eCommerce businesses to access its infrastructure across 31 countries in the European Economic Area under its own licence.

The expansion marks a new phase for Getnet in Europe, extending its successful presence in Latin America, Spain and Portugal to a broader global proposition focused on pan-European multinational merchants, Marketplaces and eCommerce businesses.

As European commerce becomes increasingly cross-border, businesses face the challenge of navigating multiple payment methods, regulations and technical integrations. Getnet's European platform brings these capabilities together through a single infrastructure called Getnet Single Entry Point (SEP). SEP allows merchants to connect to multiple markets through one integration while accessing leading local payment methods, including Bizum in Spain and MB WAY in Portugal, where Getnet operates as a direct member, enabling a stronger value proposition for merchants. 

The model is already operating at scale in Latin America, where Getnet processes billions of transactions across Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Uruguay, Colombia and Argentina. Its expansion into Europe brings the same multi-market approach to the region, combining a single integration with the local payment capabilities consumers expect in each market.

In addition to payment acceptance, Getnet will enable merchants, platforms and payment service providers to collect funds, reconcile balances, convert currencies and make payouts through the same infrastructure. The solution initially supports 20 currencies and payouts to 140 countries.

 

“This expansion marks a new chapter for Getnet in Europe. Businesses can access multiple markets through a single integration, with transparent pricing and dedicated support. Our goal is simple: help companies grow internationally while improving payment performance"

Ruben Justel, CEO at Getnet Europe.

Getnet is also building an open partner ecosystem across the European commerce and payments landscape, working with PSPs, technology and software providers, and other industry partners to help businesses access and scale payment capabilities across markets.

This approach is designed to evolve as commerce changes, from instant and account-to-account payments to new AI-driven purchasing models. Getnet is already advancing this next generation of commerce through agentic payments, with the completion of Latin America’s first agentic transaction using Mastercard Agent Pay.

“The future of commerce is increasingly connected, intelligent and international. Europe plays a key role in connecting businesses across regions, including Latin America, North America and Asia. By expanding our capabilities in Europe, we are creating new opportunities for merchants, platforms and partners to grow across borderss”

Juan Franco, CEO at Getnet. 

The European expansion is part of Getnet’s broader Smart Commerce strategy: making increasingly complex commerce environments simpler by connecting payment acceptance with expanding capabilities such as money management and payouts, data and intelligent decision-making fraud platforms. At the same time, Getnet is expanding beyond payments with innovative solutions designed to help merchants grow their businesses — from the recently announced Getnet Engage loyalty platform to a new generation of AI-powered in-store solutions that bring capabilities such as biometric recognition and voice interaction into the physical shopping experience.
