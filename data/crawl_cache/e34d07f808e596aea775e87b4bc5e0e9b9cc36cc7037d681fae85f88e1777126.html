Our Agentic Commerce Suite

One orchestration layer. Four complementary solutions.

Designed to support merchants at every stage of AI maturity.

MCP AI Tool Kit
Orchestrate secure agentic payments

Built on our global eCommerce API, the MCP AI Tool Kit is the infrastructure layer that connects merchants, AI agents and payment networks, enabling secure and trusted transactions across the Agentic Commerce ecosystem. 

It enables merchants to:

  • Secure capture of AI- initiated transactions
  • Integration with ecosystem security protocols such as Mastercard (Agent Pay)
  • Support for emerging standards like AP2
  • Agent identification, authentication and tokenisation
  • Full traceability and merchant-level control

Outcome: Secure, interoperable and future-ready payments for the agentic economy
