1. Data Controller

This Cookie Notice explains how Getnet Payments, S.L. ("Getnet", "we", "our" or "us"), as the controller responsible for this website www.getnet.net (the "Website"), uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or interact with the Wbebsite.

The purpose of this Cookie Notice is to provide you with clear and transparent information about:

  • the cookies and similar technologies used on the Website;
  • the purposes for which they are used;
  • the legal basis for their use;
  • the third parties that may receive information collected through those technologies;
  • how you can manage or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time.

This Cookie Notice should be read together with our Privacy Notice, which explains in greater detail how we process personal data collected through the Website and the rights available to you under applicable data protection legislation.

2. Definition of cookies

Cookies are small text files that are downloaded and stored on your device (such as your computer, smartphone or tablet) when you visit a website. Cookies enable the website to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit, such as your preferred language, your browsing preferences or previous interactions with the Website.

In addition to cookies, the Website may use similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, software development kits (SDKs), local storage or comparable technologies that perform equivalent functions. For simplicity, this Cookie Notice refers to all such technologies collectively as "cookies", unless otherwise specified.

Depending on their purpose, cookies may be used to:

  • ensure the Website functions correctly;
  • remember your preferences;
  • measure and improve the performance of the Website;
  • analyse how visitors use the Website;
  • personalise content;
  • measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns; 
  • display personalised advertising where you have provided your consent.

Some cookies are placed directly by Getnet (first-party cookies), while others are placed by carefully selected third-party providers that deliver services on our behalf or provide embedded content or advertising functionality (third-party cookies).

3. Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies for different purposes depending on their function.

These purposes include:

a) Ensuring that the Website operates correctly

Certain cookies are essential for the operation, security and functionality of the Website.

Without these cookies, some parts of the Website may not function correctly.

b) Remembering your preferences

Cookies allow us to remember choices you have made, such as your language preferences or cookie settings, so that you do not have to make those selections each time you visit the Website.

c) Measuring and improving Website performance

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our Website, including:

  • which pages are visited most frequently;
  • how users navigate through the Website;
  • how long visitors remain on particular pages;
  • whether users encounter technical issues.

This information allows us to improve the performance, usability and accessibility of our Website.

Where required by law, these cookies are only placed after you have provided your consent.

d) Measuring the effectiveness of our communications and advertising

Where you consent, certain cookies allow us to understand whether our advertising campaigns are effective.

These cookies may measure:

  • visits originating from advertising campaigns;
  • interactions with online advertisements;
  • conversions;
  • aggregated campaign performance.

e) Delivering personalised content and advertising

Subject to your consent, certain third-party providers may use cookies to display advertisements that are more relevant to your interests based on your browsing behaviour across websites.

These cookies may also be used to limit the number of times you see a particular advertisement and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

4. Legal basis for the use of cookies

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) (“GDPR”) and the Spanish Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce Act (“LSSI”), the legal basis for the use of cookies depends on the type of cookie and the purposes for which it is used.

a) Strictly necessary cookies

  • Strictly necessary cookies are essential for the operation of the Website or for providing a service expressly requested by you.
  • These cookies do not require your consent.
  • They are used solely on the basis that they are necessary to provide the service requested by the user, in accordance with Article 22.2 of the LSSI.

b) All other cookies

Analytics, functionality, social media and advertising cookies are only placed on your device after you have provided your prior consent through the Privacy Preference Center (our Cookie Banner).

You are free to:

  • accept all optional cookies;
  • reject all optional cookies; or
  • choose the specific categories of cookies you wish to accept.

Your decision will not affect your ability to access the Website, although rejecting certain categories of cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some optional features.

You may withdraw or modify your consent at any time by clicking the Cookie Settings link available on the Website to open our Cookie Banner.

The withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before such withdrawal.

5. Consent and management of cookie preferences

When you first visit the Website, you will be presented with our Cookie Banner that allows you to:

  • accept all optional cookies;
  • reject all optional cookies; or
  • manage your preferences by selecting the categories of cookies you wish to allow.

Except for strictly necessary cookies, no cookies will be placed on your device unless and until you have provided your consent.

You may withdraw or modify your consent at any time by clicking the Cookie Settings link provided below in this Cookie Notice to open our Cookie Banner.

Strictly necessary cookies do not require your consent because they are essential for the operation of the Website or for providing a service expressly requested by you, in accordance with Article 22.2 of the LSSI.
